Dual-Target Selectivity Study — Expanded Target Pairs (v4)

Last updated: 2026-02-25 • This list is meant for methodological benchmarking (selectivity vs dual-target) across diverse protein classes. (v4)
ChEMBL mini-stats under each target show counts of activity records by standard type (IC50, Ki, Kd) and total; values refreshed from verification cache/API on 2026-02-25.

Purpose

Curated pairs of highly pocket-similar targets from different protein classes (kinases, GPCRs, oxidoreductases, proteases, nuclear receptors, etc.). Designed for experiments where you want to demonstrate that the same workflow can recover:

  • Selective compounds when pockets are similar but not identical
  • Dual-target compounds when pockets are nearly indistinguishable

Inclusion criteria (practical)

  • Pocket similarity: estimated ≥53% (or literature-consistent “high pocket conservation”).
  • Structures: human co-crystal structures, resolution ≤2.5 Å (PDB codes linked).
  • Reference ligand: at least one clinically approved drug with known selectivity/dual profile (FDA status shown).
  • Diversity: expanded beyond the original therapeutic buckets; now spans 10+ protein classes.

Notes on selectivity numbers

“Selectivity” is reported as an approximate fold preference for the indicated target (or “near-dual”), and is assay-dependent. Where I corrected obvious mismatches (e.g., safinamide and rivastigmine), the updated values are based on published IC50/Ki data.

Quick sanity-checks (what was changed vs the earlier draft)

  • Added new protein classes: GPCR, phosphodiesterase, carbonic anhydrase, serine protease, PARP transferases, HDAC epigenetic enzymes.
  • Added FDA-status column (and flagged the non-FDA saroglitazar case).
  • Corrected selectivity notes where the earlier values were obviously too low for well-known selective drugs.
  • Unified styling (badges, links, consistent wording).

Pocket similarity is now computed from co-crystal PDB structures using residues within 5 A of the bound ligand. Values are sequence-identity percentages of those ligand-contacting pocket residues.
