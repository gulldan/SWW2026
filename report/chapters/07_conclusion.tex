This study investigated whether dual-target reinforcement-learning
generation with a worst-case scalarization reward improves dual hit-rate
relative to single-target baselines, as evaluated by an orthogonal learned
affinity surrogate (Boltz-2).  The experimental design---pre-registered
pairs, fixed budgets, hierarchical bootstrap with FDR correction, and
a conjunctive significance criterion---was chosen to minimize the risk
of over-claiming.

Three methodological contributions extend beyond the specific hypothesis:
(1)~the systematic exploration of a continuous reward-weight space unifies
target selectivity and polypharmacology within a single parametric framework;
(2)~orthogonal evaluation (UniDock training, Boltz-2 evaluation) avoids the
circular evaluation common in generative drug design;
(3)~the fully automated eight-stage pipeline produces reproducible,
auditable results from configuration files alone.

\subsection*{Future Work}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Independent cross-validation:} GNINA CNN re-scoring of
  generated poses would add a third independent evaluation layer.
\item \textbf{Binding stability:} molecular dynamics simulations
  (GROMACS, $\geq$50\,ns) for top dual-hit candidates to assess
  binding-mode stability beyond static scoring.
\item \textbf{Pareto-based generation:} replacing the worst-case
  scalarization with Pareto-based RL methods
  (e.g., PMMG~\citep{pmmg2025}, ParetoDrug~\citep{paretodrug2024})
  could improve Pareto-front coverage at the cost of additional
  architectural complexity.
\item \textbf{Synthesis-aware generation:} coupling dual-target
  rewards with synthesis-constrained generators such as
  SynFlowNet~\citep{cretu2025synflownet} or
  RxnFlow~\citep{lee2025rxnflow} would produce immediately
  synthesizable dual-active candidates.
\item \textbf{Increased statistical power:} expanding to
  $\geq$10 seeds and applying wild cluster
  bootstrap~\citep{cameron2008bootstrap} would tighten confidence
  intervals and strengthen $p$-value reliability.
\item \textbf{Experimental validation:} synthesis and IC$_{50}$
  measurement of top-ranked dual hits is the ultimate test of
  the computational predictions.
\end{itemize}
